Lease Issues List

Reads a commercial office lease against a landlord's standard positions and drafts the issues list a lawyer reviews before anything goes back to the other side. Every quote is checked word for word against the lease.

How it reads a lease

One Claude call reads the whole lease against the firm's playbook and drafts a call, a quote and a requested change for every position. Code, not the model, decides what reaches the lawyer: a quote that is not in the lease word for word is removed, and any call left without a verified quote, marked low confidence, or about a point the lease never mentions becomes a check item instead of an issue.

Read

The whole lease, riders included, against 15 standard positions.

Quote

Each call cites the section and quotes the clause word for word.

Check

Quotes are matched against the lease text. Anything unverified goes to a lawyer.

Draft

An issues list in Word, with a requested change for each departure.

The playbook

A sample landlord playbook for a Manhattan office lease, written for this demo from terms that commonly appear in New York office leases. It is not any firm's playbook and not legal advice; a firm swaps in its own positions as a YAML file.

All 15 standard positions
PositionSeverityStandard position
Assignment and sublettingcriticalTenant may not assign the lease or sublet any part of the premises without Landlord's prior written consent. Before consenting, Landlord may take back (recapture) the space. If Tenant sublets or assigns for more than it pays, Landlord receives at least half of the profit.
Permitted usemajorThe premises are used only for general, executive and administrative offices, with no change of use without Landlord's consent.
AlterationsmajorTenant needs Landlord's prior consent for all alterations except purely decorative work (painting, carpeting), uses contractors Landlord approves, and pays Landlord's reasonable review costs.
Surrender and restorationmajorAt the end of the term Tenant removes its specialty alterations (for example vaults, raised floors, internal staircases, supplemental HVAC) and repairs the damage at its cost.
Operating expense escalationscriticalTenant pays its proportionate share of increases in operating expenses over a base year, with no cap on controllable expenses.
Real estate tax escalationsmajorTenant pays its proportionate share of increases in real estate taxes over a base tax year.
Security depositcriticalTenant posts cash or an unconditional letter of credit, Landlord may draw on it after a default, and any reduction (burn-down) applies only if Tenant has never been in default.
Tenant's insurancemajorTenant carries commercial general liability insurance of at least 5 million dollars per occurrence (primary plus umbrella), names Landlord and its managing agent as additional insureds, and both parties waive subrogation.
IndemnificationmajorTenant indemnifies Landlord for claims arising from Tenant's use of the premises and its people. Landlord's indemnity, if any, covers only its negligence or willful misconduct.
Limit on Landlord's liabilitycriticalLandlord's liability is limited to its interest in the building, and Landlord is never liable for consequential damages.
Tenant default and cure periodscriticalTenant has about 5 to 10 days after notice to cure a failure to pay rent, and 30 days after notice to cure other defaults, extended only while Tenant diligently pursues a cure.
Fire or other casualtymajorLandlord restores the building and premises after a casualty. Tenant may terminate only if restoration is not finished within a long outside period (12 months or more), and rent abates only for the unusable space.
Holding overmajorIf Tenant stays after the term ends, it pays at least 150 percent of the last rent, rising over time, and is liable for Landlord's damages if it stays past a short grace period after notice.
Renewal, expansion and first-offer rightsmajorTenant has no renewal, expansion, first-offer or early termination rights unless Landlord approves them, and any renewal is at fair market rent.
Governing law and jury waiverminorNew York law governs, and both parties waive a jury trial.

Run it yourself

Python 3.10+. Replays the recorded review; a new lease needs an Anthropic API key.

pip install -e .
lease-review lease.txt --out issues.docx
