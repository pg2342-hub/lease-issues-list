AGREEMENT OF LEASE

THIS AGREEMENT OF LEASE is made between Halden Owner LLC ("Landlord") and Coalport Media Group LLC ("Tenant").
All names, addresses and figures in this lease are invented for testing.

ARTICLE 1. PREMISES

1.1 Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant hires from Landlord, the entire twelfth floor (the "Premises") of the building known as 1150 Wexford Place, New York, New York (the "Building").

ARTICLE 2. TERM

2.1 The term of this Lease (the "Term") begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the last day of the month in which the tenth anniversary of the Commencement Date falls (the "Expiration Date").

ARTICLE 3. FIXED RENT

3.1 Tenant shall pay Fixed Rent at the annual rate set out in Schedule A, in equal monthly installments in advance on the first day of each month.

ARTICLE 4. SERVICES

4.1 Landlord shall provide passenger elevator service, heat and air conditioning during Business Hours, and cleaning of the Premises in accordance with Schedule B.

ARTICLE 5. ALTERATIONS

5.1 Tenant shall make no alterations, additions or improvements to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, except decorative work such as painting and carpeting. All work shall be performed by contractors approved by Landlord, and Tenant shall reimburse Landlord for its reasonable out-of-pocket costs of reviewing Tenant's plans.

ARTICLE 6. END OF TERM

6.1 On the expiration or earlier termination of the Term, Tenant shall, at its expense, remove all Specialty Alterations (including vaults, raised floors, internal staircases and supplemental air conditioning units) and repair any damage caused by their removal, whether or not Landlord designated them for removal when it consented to them.

ARTICLE 7. OPERATING EXPENSES

7.1 Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, Tenant's Proportionate Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses for each Operating Year exceed Operating Expenses for the Base Operating Year, provided that Controllable Operating Expenses included in that calculation shall not increase by more than three percent (3%) per year on a cumulative basis.

ARTICLE 8. TAXES

8.1 Tenant shall have no obligation to pay any share of Real Estate Taxes, which are included in the Fixed Rent.

ARTICLE 9. SECURITY

9.1 On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deliver to Landlord an unconditional, irrevocable letter of credit in the amount of the Security Amount. The Security Amount shall be reduced by one-half on the second anniversary of the Commencement Date, whether or not any default has previously occurred.

ARTICLE 10. INSURANCE

10.1 Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than Five Million Dollars ($5,000,000) per occurrence, which may be met with primary and umbrella policies, naming Landlord and Landlord's managing agent as additional insureds. Landlord and Tenant each waive all rights of recovery against the other to the extent of insurance proceeds received, and each shall obtain a waiver of subrogation from its insurers.

ARTICLE 11. INDEMNITY

11.1 Landlord shall indemnify and hold Tenant harmless from all claims arising from any occurrence in the Building or its common areas. Tenant shall indemnify Landlord only for claims arising from Tenant's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

ARTICLE 12. LANDLORD'S LIABILITY

12.1 Landlord shall be liable to Tenant for all damages, including consequential damages and lost profits, arising from Landlord's breach of this Lease.

ARTICLE 13. DEFAULT

13.1 Each of the following is an Event of Default: (a) Tenant fails to pay any Rent within thirty (30) days after a second written notice from Landlord; or (b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation within sixty (60) days after notice from Landlord.

ARTICLE 14. FIRE OR OTHER CASUALTY

14.1 If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall restore the Building and the Premises. Rent shall abate in proportion to the part of the Premises rendered unusable. If Landlord has not substantially completed the restoration within fifteen (15) months after the casualty, Tenant may terminate this Lease by notice to Landlord.

ARTICLE 15. HOLDING OVER

15.1 If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term, Tenant shall pay for each month of holdover one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Rent for the last month of the Term for the first sixty (60) days, and two hundred percent (200%) thereafter. If Tenant remains in possession more than thirty (30) days after notice from Landlord, Tenant shall also be liable for all damages Landlord suffers, including consequential damages.

ARTICLE 16. OPTIONS

16.1 Tenant shall have no option to renew or extend the Term, no right to lease additional space in the Building and no right to terminate this Lease before the Expiration Date.

ARTICLE 17. GOVERNING LAW; JURY WAIVER

17.1 This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
