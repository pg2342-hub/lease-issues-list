AGREEMENT OF LEASE

THIS AGREEMENT OF LEASE is made between Halden Owner LLC ("Landlord") and Harbor Vale Analytics LLC ("Tenant").
All names, addresses and figures in this lease are invented for testing.

ARTICLE 1. PREMISES

1.1 Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant hires from Landlord, the entire fourth floor (the "Premises") of the building known as 412 Halden Street, New York, New York (the "Building").

ARTICLE 2. TERM

2.1 The term of this Lease (the "Term") begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the last day of the month in which the tenth anniversary of the Commencement Date falls (the "Expiration Date").

ARTICLE 3. FIXED RENT

3.1 Tenant shall pay Fixed Rent at the annual rate set out in Schedule A, in equal monthly installments in advance on the first day of each month.

ARTICLE 4. SERVICES

4.1 Landlord shall provide passenger elevator service, heat and air conditioning during Business Hours, and cleaning of the Premises in accordance with Schedule B.

ARTICLE 5. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

5.1 Tenant may assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises with the consent of Landlord, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Landlord shall have no right to recapture the Premises, and Tenant shall be entitled to retain any rent or other consideration received from an assignee or subtenant.

ARTICLE 6. USE

6.1 Tenant may use the Premises for any lawful purpose.

ARTICLE 7. ALTERATIONS

7.1 Tenant shall make no alterations, additions or improvements to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, except decorative work such as painting and carpeting. All work shall be performed by contractors approved by Landlord, and Tenant shall reimburse Landlord for its reasonable out-of-pocket costs of reviewing Tenant's plans.

ARTICLE 8. END OF TERM

8.1 On the expiration or earlier termination of the Term, Tenant shall, at its expense, remove all Specialty Alterations (including vaults, raised floors, internal staircases and supplemental air conditioning units) and repair any damage caused by their removal, whether or not Landlord designated them for removal when it consented to them.

ARTICLE 9. OPERATING EXPENSES

9.1 Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, Tenant's Proportionate Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses for each Operating Year exceed Operating Expenses for the Base Operating Year, provided that Controllable Operating Expenses included in that calculation shall not increase by more than three percent (3%) per year on a cumulative basis.

ARTICLE 10. TAXES

10.1 Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, Tenant's Proportionate Share of the amount by which Real Estate Taxes for each Tax Year exceed Real Estate Taxes for the Base Tax Year.

ARTICLE 11. SECURITY

11.1 On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deliver to Landlord an unconditional, irrevocable letter of credit in the amount of the Security Amount. The Security Amount shall be reduced by one-half on the second anniversary of the Commencement Date, whether or not any default has previously occurred.

ARTICLE 12. INSURANCE

12.1 Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than Five Million Dollars ($5,000,000) per occurrence, which may be met with primary and umbrella policies, naming Landlord and Landlord's managing agent as additional insureds. Landlord and Tenant each waive all rights of recovery against the other to the extent of insurance proceeds received, and each shall obtain a waiver of subrogation from its insurers.

ARTICLE 13. INDEMNITY

13.1 Landlord shall indemnify and hold Tenant harmless from all claims arising from any occurrence in the Building or its common areas. Tenant shall indemnify Landlord only for claims arising from Tenant's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

ARTICLE 14. LANDLORD'S LIABILITY

14.1 Landlord shall be liable to Tenant for all damages, including consequential damages and lost profits, arising from Landlord's breach of this Lease.

ARTICLE 15. FIRE OR OTHER CASUALTY

15.1 If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty and Landlord's architect estimates that restoration will take more than one hundred twenty (120) days, Tenant may terminate this Lease by notice to Landlord. Rent shall abate in full until the restoration is complete.

ARTICLE 16. HOLDING OVER

16.1 If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term, Tenant shall pay for each month of holdover one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Rent for the last month of the Term for the first sixty (60) days, and two hundred percent (200%) thereafter. If Tenant remains in possession more than thirty (30) days after notice from Landlord, Tenant shall also be liable for all damages Landlord suffers, including consequential damages.

ARTICLE 17. OPTIONS

17.1 Tenant shall have one option to extend the Term for five (5) years at ninety percent (90%) of the Fixed Rent payable in the last year of the initial Term, and a right of first offer on any space that becomes available on the floor above the Premises.

ARTICLE 18. GOVERNING LAW; JURY WAIVER

18.1 This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
