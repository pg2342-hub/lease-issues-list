AGREEMENT OF LEASE

THIS AGREEMENT OF LEASE is made between Halden Owner LLC ("Landlord") and Northline Software Corp. ("Tenant").
All names, addresses and figures in this lease are invented for testing.

ARTICLE 1. PREMISES

1.1 Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant hires from Landlord, the entire twentieth floor (the "Premises") of the building known as 88 Bellmore Plaza, New York, New York (the "Building").

ARTICLE 2. TERM

2.1 The term of this Lease (the "Term") begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the last day of the month in which the tenth anniversary of the Commencement Date falls (the "Expiration Date").

ARTICLE 3. FIXED RENT

3.1 Tenant shall pay Fixed Rent at the annual rate set out in Schedule A, in equal monthly installments in advance on the first day of each month.

ARTICLE 4. SERVICES

4.1 Landlord shall provide passenger elevator service, heat and air conditioning during Business Hours, and cleaning of the Premises in accordance with Schedule B.

ARTICLE 5. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

5.1 Tenant may assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises with the consent of Landlord, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Landlord shall have no right to recapture the Premises, and Tenant shall be entitled to retain any rent or other consideration received from an assignee or subtenant.

ARTICLE 6. USE

6.1 Tenant may use the Premises for any lawful purpose.

ARTICLE 7. ALTERATIONS

7.1 Tenant may make non-structural alterations that do not affect the Building systems without Landlord's consent, provided that the cost of any single project does not exceed Two Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($250,000). Landlord's consent to any other alteration shall not be unreasonably withheld.

ARTICLE 8. OPERATING EXPENSES

8.1 Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, Tenant's Proportionate Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses for each Operating Year exceed Operating Expenses for the Base Operating Year, provided that Controllable Operating Expenses included in that calculation shall not increase by more than three percent (3%) per year on a cumulative basis.

ARTICLE 9. SECURITY

9.1 On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deliver to Landlord an unconditional, irrevocable letter of credit in the amount of the Security Amount. Landlord may draw on the letter of credit after any Event of Default. The Security Amount shall be reduced on the third anniversary of the Commencement Date, provided that no Event of Default has ever occurred under this Lease.

ARTICLE 10. INSURANCE

10.1 Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000) per occurrence. Landlord waives all rights of recovery against Tenant for any loss covered by Landlord's property insurance.

ARTICLE 11. LANDLORD'S LIABILITY

11.1 Landlord shall be liable to Tenant for all damages, including consequential damages and lost profits, arising from Landlord's breach of this Lease.

ARTICLE 12. FIRE OR OTHER CASUALTY

12.1 If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall restore the Building and the Premises. Rent shall abate in proportion to the part of the Premises rendered unusable. If Landlord has not substantially completed the restoration within fifteen (15) months after the casualty, Tenant may terminate this Lease by notice to Landlord.

ARTICLE 13. HOLDING OVER

13.1 If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term, Tenant shall pay for each month of holdover the Rent in effect for the last month of the Term, and Tenant shall not be liable for any other damages.

ARTICLE 14. OPTIONS

14.1 Tenant shall have no option to renew or extend the Term, no right to lease additional space in the Building and no right to terminate this Lease before the Expiration Date.

ARTICLE 15. GOVERNING LAW; JURY WAIVER

15.1 This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
