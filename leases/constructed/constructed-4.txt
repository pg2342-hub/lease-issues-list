AGREEMENT OF LEASE

THIS AGREEMENT OF LEASE is made between Halden Owner LLC ("Landlord") and Ashgrove Partners LP ("Tenant").
All names, addresses and figures in this lease are invented for testing.

ARTICLE 1. PREMISES

1.1 Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant hires from Landlord, the entire sixth floor (the "Premises") of the building known as 23 Tamsin Lane, New York, New York (the "Building").

ARTICLE 2. TERM

2.1 The term of this Lease (the "Term") begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the last day of the month in which the tenth anniversary of the Commencement Date falls (the "Expiration Date").

ARTICLE 3. FIXED RENT

3.1 Tenant shall pay Fixed Rent at the annual rate set out in Schedule A, in equal monthly installments in advance on the first day of each month.

ARTICLE 4. SERVICES

4.1 Landlord shall provide passenger elevator service, heat and air conditioning during Business Hours, and cleaning of the Premises in accordance with Schedule B.

ARTICLE 5. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

5.1 Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without the prior written consent of Landlord in each instance. Before granting consent, Landlord may elect, by notice to Tenant within thirty (30) days after Tenant's request, to recapture the space proposed to be assigned or sublet. If Tenant receives rent or other consideration in excess of the Rent payable hereunder, Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of such excess.

ARTICLE 6. USE

6.1 Tenant may use the Premises for any lawful purpose.

ARTICLE 7. ALTERATIONS

7.1 Tenant shall make no alterations, additions or improvements to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, except decorative work such as painting and carpeting. All work shall be performed by contractors approved by Landlord, and Tenant shall reimburse Landlord for its reasonable out-of-pocket costs of reviewing Tenant's plans.

ARTICLE 8. END OF TERM

8.1 Tenant shall have no obligation to remove any alterations or to restore the Premises at the end of the Term, other than items Landlord designated for removal in writing at the time it approved the plans for them.

ARTICLE 9. OPERATING EXPENSES

9.1 Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, Tenant's Proportionate Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses for each Operating Year exceed Operating Expenses for the Base Operating Year, provided that Controllable Operating Expenses included in that calculation shall not increase by more than three percent (3%) per year on a cumulative basis.

ARTICLE 10. TAXES

10.1 Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, Tenant's Proportionate Share of the amount by which Real Estate Taxes for each Tax Year exceed Real Estate Taxes for the Base Tax Year.

ARTICLE 11. SECURITY

11.1 On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deliver to Landlord an unconditional, irrevocable letter of credit in the amount of the Security Amount. Landlord may draw on the letter of credit after any Event of Default. The Security Amount shall be reduced on the third anniversary of the Commencement Date, provided that no Event of Default has ever occurred under this Lease.

ARTICLE 12. INSURANCE

12.1 Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000) per occurrence. Landlord waives all rights of recovery against Tenant for any loss covered by Landlord's property insurance.

ARTICLE 13. INDEMNITY

13.1 Tenant shall indemnify and hold Landlord harmless from all claims arising from the use or occupancy of the Premises or from any act or omission of Tenant or its employees, agents, contractors or invitees. Landlord shall indemnify Tenant only for claims arising from Landlord's negligence or willful misconduct.

ARTICLE 14. LANDLORD'S LIABILITY

14.1 Tenant shall look solely to Landlord's interest in the Building for the recovery of any judgment against Landlord, and no other property or assets of Landlord or its principals shall be subject to levy. In no event shall Landlord be liable for consequential, indirect or special damages.

ARTICLE 15. FIRE OR OTHER CASUALTY

15.1 If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty and Landlord's architect estimates that restoration will take more than one hundred twenty (120) days, Tenant may terminate this Lease by notice to Landlord. Rent shall abate in full until the restoration is complete.

ARTICLE 16. HOLDING OVER

16.1 If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term, Tenant shall pay for each month of holdover the Rent in effect for the last month of the Term, and Tenant shall not be liable for any other damages.

ARTICLE 17. OPTIONS

17.1 Tenant shall have one option to extend the Term for five (5) years at ninety percent (90%) of the Fixed Rent payable in the last year of the initial Term, and a right of first offer on any space that becomes available on the floor above the Premises.

ARTICLE 18. GOVERNING LAW; JURY WAIVER

18.1 This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
